MULTI-CARB PROTOCOL RECOMMENDED
For sessions over 2.5 hrs consuming 60g+/hr, use a 2:1 glucose:fructose ratio. This saturates separate intestinal transporters, maximising absorption up to 90g/hr (Jeukendrup 2011).
INTRA-WORKOUT OPTIONS
💡 Sip fluid regularly, not all at once. Sodium in sports drinks helps fluid retention and maintains the drive to drink.

IDEAL POST-WORKOUT FOODS
